Course 
Slide 3 — What Is This Course About?
Course Goal

This course focuses on:

Data mining and Machine learning for large scale datasets i.e. too large for a single machine

Question
How do we make data mining and machine learning work when the data no longer fits on one computer?
---

Slide 4 — Big Data Analytics

Machine learning and deep learning have achieved remarkable results.

But successful ML/DL systems often depend on:

Big Data
Compute

More data + more compute → increasingly capable models and systems

The algorithms themselves are not necessarily new.

Many fundamental ideas behind modern ML were developed decades ago.

What changed?

Data became enormous.
GPUs and distributed systems became widely available.
Organizations began collecting data at unprecedented scale.

Slide — Research Project
Team-Based Research Project

Team size: 4 students

Your project should:

Use a large dataset, preferably with millions of records.
Address a meaningful real world problem.
Cannot be the same as your FYP idea.
Divide the problem into 4 meaningful subproblems.
Discuss in a meeting to refine the idea.
Research Process

Problem → Subproblems → Literature Review → Baselines → Proposed Method → Experiments → Results

Slide  — Research Project: Expectations
Every Student Must Own a Subproblem

For each subproblem:

Identify the problem.
Study existing approaches.
Implement baseline methods.
Develop/propose an improved approach.
Run experiments.
Compare results.
Present findings.
Timeline

By mid-semester break

Baselines should be running.

Later

Proposed methods + experiments + results.

Research Opportunity

Strong projects may be refined into:

A research paper

Good research experience can be valuable for graduate studies.
