01 — Foundation

Scrum turns uncertainty
into learning.

Instead of attempting to design and build everything at once, Scrum creates short feedback cycles. The team creates a valuable Increment, inspects what happened, learns from stakeholders and adapts what comes next.

02 — Accountabilities

Three accountabilities.
One Scrum Team.

Scrum defines accountabilities rather than traditional command-and-control job titles. Each accountability contributes to creating value and achieving the Product Goal.

◈

Product Owner

The Product Owner is accountable for maximizing the value of the product resulting from the work of the Scrum Team.

Value & Product Goal
✦

Scrum Master

The Scrum Master is accountable for establishing Scrum as defined in the Scrum Guide and helping everyone understand Scrum theory and practice.

Effectiveness & Scrum
◆

Developers

Developers are accountable for creating any aspect of a usable Increment each Sprint and for creating the Sprint Backlog, including the plan for the Sprint.

Increment & Quality

One month or less

04 — The Three Artifacts

Transparency through
artifacts & commitments.

Each Scrum artifact contains information that makes important work visible. Each artifact also has a corresponding commitment that provides focus and direction.

01

Product Backlog

The Product Backlog is an emergent, ordered list of what is needed to improve the product. Its commitment is the Product Goal.

02

Sprint Backlog

The Sprint Backlog consists of the Sprint Goal, the set of Product Backlog Items selected for the Sprint, and the actionable plan for delivering the Increment. Its commitment is the Sprint Goal.

03

Increment

The Increment is a concrete stepping stone toward the Product Goal. Its commitment is the Definition of Done.

06 — Quality

What does
"Done" mean?

The Definition of Done is a formal description of the state of the Increment when it meets the quality measures required for the product.

Definition of Done

When a Product Backlog Item meets the Definition of Done, an Increment is born. If it does not meet the Definition of Done, it is not part of the Increment and should not be released or presented as complete.

✓ Meets the agreed quality criteria
✓ Meets the organization's Definition of Done
✓ Creates a usable state of the product
✓ Supports transparency about Increment quality
