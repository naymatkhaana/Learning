Cosine Similarity

Cosine of the angle

cos θ = a · b / (‖a‖ ‖b‖)

The dot product tells us how strongly the two vectors point in the same direction. Cosine similarity focuses only on their direction, not their size.

cos θ = 0

Dot product: multiply and sum

Each corresponding pair is multiplied, and then all those products are added together.

We usually assume a and b are unit vectors and therefore:

cos θ = a · b
cosine similarity = dot product

What Shape Projects a Vector?

Suppose we have a vector with shape 2 × 1. What matrix is required to transform it into a 3 × 1 vector?

2 × 2

Can it produce 3 × 1?

3 × 2

Can it produce 3 × 1?

2 × 3

Can it produce 3 × 1?

(3 × 2)(2 × 1) = 3 × 1

Therefore, to map 2-dimensional → 3-dimensional, we need a matrix of shape 3 × 2.

Now suppose we want to map a 2 × 1 vector into a 5 × 1 vector.

(5 × 2)(2 × 1) = 5 × 1
Required projection matrix:
5 × 2
